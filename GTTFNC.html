Honor & Tradition

"As part of its educational philosophy, the Global Traditional Taekwondo Federation (GTTF) encourages students to uphold a set of "Do" Principles known as
10 Taekwondo commitments (Do-hun).
These commitments are recited by students regularly and serve as a moral and behavioral guide both inside and outside the training hall (dojang). They are:"

  • 1. I will be loyal to my country.
    Patriotic and resolute
  • 2. I will honor my parents.
    Respectful and grateful
  • 3. I will love my family and cooperate with them.
    Warm and caring
  • 4. I will be faithful to my friends.
    Loyal and trustworthy
  • 5. I will respect my elders and care for my juniors.
    Wise and nurturing
  • 6. I will establish positive relationships with my teachers.
    Humble and eager to learn
  • 7. I will treat all living things with care.
    Compassionate and gentle
  • 8. I will never seek advantage of those who are weaker.
    Strong and protective
  • 9. I will only use Taekwondo to benefit myself and others.
    Determined and righteous
  • 10. I will always finish what I start.
    Persistent and disciplined
